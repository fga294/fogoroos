Botafogo de Futebol e Regatas

Founded in 1904 as Botafogo Football Club and merged with Club de Regatas Botafogo in 1942, Botafogo FR is one of Rio de Janeiro's 'Big Four'. Known as the Lone Star (Estrela Solitária), the black-and-white striped club has a rich history of triumphs, including 3 Brazilian Série A titles (1968, 1995, 2024), 1 Copa Libertadores (2024), and 21 Campeonato Carioca wins. Legends like Garrincha, Nílton Santos, and Didi propelled them to glory, including World Cup successes with Brazil.

Home to Estádio Olímpico Nilton Santos, Botafogo embodies resilience, from unbeaten streaks to recent SAF revival under Eagle Holdings. Glória eterna ao Glorioso!
